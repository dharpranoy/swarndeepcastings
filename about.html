ABOUT US

Swarndeep Castings Private Limited, incorporated on 11th June 2018, is a leader in the casting industry with a strong focus on serving the railway sector. Our expertise lies in manufacturing high-quality locomotive parts, which are essential to the efficient operation of rail networks. With over a decade of experience, we have become synonymous with precision engineering and innovative technology, particularly in the railway industry.

Our state-of-the-art facilities and skilled team enable us to produce castings that meet the stringent demands of the railway sector, ensuring top-notch quality and durability. While railways remain our primary focus, we also cater to automotive, industrial, and custom applications, offering reliable and efficient casting solutions across various industries.

our diversified business sectors

Manpasand Agro Food, founded in 1995 by Mr. Ramavatar Bajoria and his sons, offers a wide range of fresh and high-quality Gota, Grounded, and Blended Spices. Their passion for delivering the finest spices led them to explore India, gaining expertise in sourcing and processing top-quality products. Over time, as consumer demand grew, Manpasand expanded its offerings while maintaining an unwavering commitment to quality. The company values its strong relationships with dealers, distributors, and C/F agents, ensuring a successful and reliable business network.
